Fusion of States 1 to 24, how will it turn out?

La ReiNa

States 1 to 24 will merge starting September 1, in a process that will take place between 4:00 and 10:00 server time (ST), according to an announcement issued by the game team on August 29 at 15:00 ST.


The first round, scheduled for 4:00 ST, will merge State 1 with 4, with State 1 remaining as the main one, while State 3 will merge into 5, with the latter remaining active. At 5:30 ST, States 15 and 18 will merge, with State 15 remaining, as well as States 19 and 20, continuing under State 20. By 7:00 ST, it will be the turn of States 21 and 22, with State 21 remaining, as well as States 16 and 24, with State 16 remaining active. Finally, at 8:30 ST, State 14 will absorb State 17, and State 13 will absorb State 23.


The announcement specifies that each phase will last approximately 90 minutes, during which players will not be able to log in. After the process, survivors will be relocated around their alliance’s rally point, and the capitals, cities, armories, and presidential positions of the surviving States will be preserved.


Likewise, arena points will be transferred to the new State, world bosses such as Frankenstein and the Biomutant will be relocated, purchased packs will be reissued, and certain progress, such as battle pass progress, will be reset to fit the new environment.


According to the announcement, players involved in this reorganization will receive rewards as compensation for the process.
